# Standard Operating Procedure: Casing Layer Preparation, Pasteurization, & Application (SOP-302)

* **Document ID:** SOP-302
* **Version:** 1.0
* **Target Region:** Global / Cultivation Facilities
* **Applies To:** Casing Preparation Technicians, Growers

---

## 1. Purpose & Scope
This SOP dictates the preparation, pasteurization, chemical balancing, and surface application of the casing layer required for *Agaricus bisporus* fruit body initiation.

---

## 2. Casing Formulation & pH Adjustment
1. **Material Blending:** Mix sphagnum peat moss, neutralized spent mushroom compost, or approved loam blends to create a porous, moisture-retentive medium.
2. **pH Buffering:** Incorporate agricultural limestone ($\mathrm{CaCO}_3$) thoroughly until the mixture reaches a stable alkaline target range of **$\text{pH } 7.2 - 7.5$**.

---

## 3. Pasteurization & Application Mechanics
1. **Mild Pasteurization:** Heat-treat or steam-pasteurize the cased mix at $60^\circ\text{C}$ for 4–6 hours to eliminate harmful pathogens while preserving beneficial microbes.
2. **Uniform Application:** Spread a consistent 3–5 cm layer uniformly over fully colonized compost blocks or trays.
3. **Ruffling (Scratching):** Once the white mycelium knits into the casing layer, lightly ruffle the surface to encourage balanced, simultaneous pinhead development.

---

## 4. Quality Control Audit
* [ ] Casing mixture pH verified via meter or strips ($7.2 - 7.5$).
* [ ] Casing moisture content optimized prior to spreading.
* [ ] Uniform depth (3–5 cm) maintained across all beds or bags.
