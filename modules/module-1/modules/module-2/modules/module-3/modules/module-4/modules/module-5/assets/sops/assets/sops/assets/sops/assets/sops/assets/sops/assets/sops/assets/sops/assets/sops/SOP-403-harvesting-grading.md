# Standard Operating Procedure: Gentle Harvesting, Quality Grading, & Cold-Storage (SOP-403)

* **Document ID:** SOP-403
* **Version:** 1.0
* **Target Region:** Global / Harvesting & Packing Rooms
* **Applies To:** Harvesters, Quality Control Inspectors, Packers

---

## 1. Purpose & Scope
This SOP defines the proper manual harvesting mechanics, quality grading parameters, and immediate post-harvest cold-chain protocols to maximize product quality and shelf life.

---

## 2. Harvesting & Grading Protocol
1. **Harvesting Technique:** Use a gentle twist-and-pull motion (or clean stem trimming as specified by market standards) to lift mature mushrooms cleanly from the casing without damaging nearby pinheads.
2. **Flush Management:** Manage harvests across 3 to 4 successive flushes (breaks), harvesting on a strict daily cycle as caps reach optimal maturity.
3. **Quality Grading:** Sort harvested produce into standardized commercial grades:
   * **Button:** Closed caps, firm veil intact.
   * **Cup:** Slightly opened veil, larger cap diameter.
   * **Open/Flats:** Fully opened caps.
   * Discard or divert bruised, deformed, or diseased mushrooms to processing lines.

---

## 3. Cold-Chain Management
1. **Immediate Transfer:** Transport harvested trays immediately from warm growing rooms to cold storage.
2. **Rapid Cooling:** Utilize rapid vacuum cooling or forced-air refrigeration units to bring internal mushroom temperatures down to **$2^\circ\text{C} - 4^\circ\text{C}$ at $90\%$ RH**, instantly halting respiration.

---

## 4. Quality Control Audit
* [ ] Harvesters adhere to clean-hands and glove hygiene policies.
* [ ] Graded batches sorted accurately by size and cap closure specifications.
* [ ] Cold room temperatures verified within $2^\circ\text{C} - 4^\circ\text{C}$ prior to packaging.
