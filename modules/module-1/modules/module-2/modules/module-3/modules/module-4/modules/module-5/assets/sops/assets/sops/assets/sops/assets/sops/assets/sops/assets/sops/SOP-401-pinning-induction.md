# Standard Operating Procedure: Pinning Induction & Shock Phase Control (SOP-401)

* **Document ID:** SOP-401
* **Version:** 1.0
* **Target Region:** Global / Growing Room Operations
* **Applies To:** Climate Control Technicians, Growing Supervisors

---

## 1. Purpose & Scope
This SOP defines the environmental shock sequence required to transition beds from the vegetative spawn run / casing colonization phase into active primordia (pinhead) development.

---

## 2. Environmental Shock Execution
1. **Temperature Reduction:** Rapidly drop growing room air temperature down to **$16^\circ\text{C} - 18^\circ\text{C}$** over a 24-48 hour window.
2. **$\text{CO}_2$ Flushing:** Increase Fresh Air Exchanges (FAEs) to drop ambient carbon dioxide levels **below $1,000\text{ ppm}$**.
3. **Evaporative Misting:** Initiate controlled fine-misting to establish the surface moisture balance required for knots to swell into pins.

---

## 3. Quality Control Audit
* [ ] Air temperature confirmed stable at $16^\circ\text{C} - 18^\circ\text{C}$.
* [ ] $\text{CO}_2$ sensor reads under $1,000\text{ ppm}$.
* [ ] Relative humidity maintained at $85\% - 90\%$ without water-logging the casing surface.
