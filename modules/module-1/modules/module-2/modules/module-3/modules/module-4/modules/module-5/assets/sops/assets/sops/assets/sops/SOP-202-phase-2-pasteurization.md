# Standard Operating Procedure: Phase II Tunnel Pasteurization & Ammonia Clearance (SOP-202)

* **Document ID:** SOP-202
* **Version:** 1.0
* **Target Region:** Global / Indoor Bulking Facilities
* **Applies To:** Facility Operators, Climate Technicians

---

## 1. Purpose & Scope
This SOP outlines the protocols for Phase II indoor pasteurization, conditioning, and ammonia removal to render the substrate selective for button mushroom mycelium.

---

## 2. Tunnel Loading & Leveling Stage
1. **Bulk Loading:** Transfer Phase I compost uniformly into indoor bulking tunnels, filling to a consistent density without over-compacting.
2. **Leveling & Recirculation:** Equalize mass temperature across the entire tunnel bed to $48^\circ\text{C} - 51^\circ\text{C}$ using automated fan recirculation and controlled fresh air integration.

---

## 3. Conditioning & Pasteurization Spike
1. **Metabolic Conditioning:** Maintain compost core temperatures at $48^\circ\text{C} - 52^\circ\text{C}$ to allow thermophilic actinomycetes to consume simple sugars and convert free ammonia into microbial protein. Monitor until ambient gaseous ammonia drops below $150\text{ ppm}$.
2. **Pasteurization Spike:** Elevate compost core temperature to $58^\circ\text{C} - 62^\circ\text{C}$ for a minimum of $8 - 12$ hours to eradicate nematodes, insect pests, and competitor molds.
3. **Controlled Cool-Down:** Rapidly ramp down temperatures using filtered ambient air until compost core reaches $24^\circ\text{C} - 26^\circ\text{C}$.

---

## 4. Quality Control Audit
* [ ] Gaseous ammonia confirmed below $150\text{ ppm}$ via gas detector tubes or sensor readouts.
* [ ] Compost core pasteurization temperature held at $58^\circ\text{C} - 62^\circ\text{C}$ for $\ge 8$ hours.
* [ ] Final spawn-ready compost temperature stabilized at $24^\circ\text{C} - 26^\circ\text{C}$.
