# Standard Operating Procedure: Aseptic Spawning, Bag/Tray Filling, & Temperature Control (SOP-301)

* **Document ID:** SOP-301
* **Version:** 1.0
* **Target Region:** Global / Spawning & Incubation Zones
* **Applies To:** Inoculation Technicians, Growing Room Operators

---

## 1. Purpose & Scope
This SOP covers the aseptic handling, grain spawn mixing rates, and environmental thermal monitoring required during the critical inoculation and spawn run phases.

---

## 2. Inoculation & Filling Protocol
1. **Sanitization:** Disinfect all hoppers, mixing lines, conveyor belts, and filling equipment with food-grade sanitizing agents prior to operation.
2. **Spawn Integration:** Mix certified disease-free *Agaricus bisporus* grain spawn uniformly into cooled compost at a rate of $0.5\% - 1.0\%$ based on wet substrate weight.
3. **Container Packing:** Fill bulk growing bags or Dutch aluminum/plastic trays to targeted uniform densities, ensuring proper aeration space.

---

## 3. Spawn Run Monitoring
1. **Temperature Regulation:** Maintain room ambient air to keep internal compost block temperatures between $24^\circ\text{C} - 25^\circ\text{C}$.
2. **Thermal Maximum Limit:** Ensure internal block temperatures never exceed **$27^\circ\text{C}$** to prevent irreversible mycelial thermal death.
3. **$\text{CO}_2$ Retention:** Maintain high carbon dioxide levels ($5,000 - 10,000\text{ ppm}$) during colonization to accelerate vegetative growth.

---

## 4. Quality Control Audit
* [ ] Spawn mixing rate verified against batch weight calculations ($0.5\% - 1.0\%$).
* [ ] Daily probe readings confirm internal block temperatures stay below $27^\circ\text{C}$.
* [ ] High $\text{CO}_2$ and humidity targets locked in throughout the incubation period.
