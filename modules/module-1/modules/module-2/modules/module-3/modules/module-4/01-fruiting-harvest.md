# Module 4: Fruiting, Pests, Diseases, Harvesting, & Post-Harvest

## 1. Induction of Primordia (Pinning)
Transitioning from vegetative spawn run to fruiting requires a precise environmental shock to trigger hyphal knotting and pinhead formation.

* **Environmental Shock Sequence:**
  * **Temperature Drop:** Rapidly lower room air temperature down to $16^\circ\text{C} - 18^\circ\text{C}$.
  * **$\text{CO}_2$ Flushing:** Spike Fresh Air Exchanges (FAEs) to reduce ambient carbon dioxide levels below $1,000\text{ ppm}$.
  * **Evaporative Balance:** Apply fine-misting over the casing layer to maintain relative humidity at $85\% - 90\%$, establishing the evaporation balance required for primordia to swell into mature mushrooms.

---

## 2. Integrated Pest Management (IPM) & Hygiene
Mushroom farms are vulnerable to rapid biological contamination. A strict biosecurity regimen is essential.

* **Target Pests & Diseases:**
  * **Insects:** Sciarid flies (*Lycoriella*), Phorid flies, Cecidomyiid larvae, and mushroom mites.
  * **Fungal/Bacterial Pathogens:** Green Mold (*Trichoderma harzianum*), Bacterial Blotch (*Pseudomonas tolaasii*), and Dry Bubble (*Verticillium fungicola*).
* **Prevention & Biosecurity Protocols:**
  * Enforce strict footbath disinfection and air-lock entry procedures.
  * Maintain clean post-crop pasteurization (cook-out) protocols before cleaning out spent rooms to eliminate lingering spores.

---

## 3. Harvesting Mechanics & Grading Standards
* **Harvesting Technique:** 
  * Use a gentle twist-and-pull motion (or clean stem trimming depending on market preference) to remove mature mushrooms without disturbing adjacent developing pins.
* **Flushing & Staging:** 
  * Harvest occurs across 3 to 4 successive flushes (breaks) spaced roughly 7–10 days apart.
* **Quality Grading:** 
  * Sort harvested mushrooms into precise market categories: Cup, Button, and Closed-Cap specifications. Check for size uniformity and implement immediate bruise-prevention handling.

---

## 4. Post-Harvest Handling & Cold Chain
* **Rapid Cooling:** 
  * Immediately transfer harvested mushrooms into rapid vacuum coolers or forced-air cold storage rooms set to $2^\circ\text{C} - 4^\circ\text{C}$ at $90\%$ RH to instantly halt metabolic respiration.
* **Packaging:** 
  * Pack into micro-perforated films or ventilated clamshell containers to prevent moisture accumulation and maximize retail shelf life.
