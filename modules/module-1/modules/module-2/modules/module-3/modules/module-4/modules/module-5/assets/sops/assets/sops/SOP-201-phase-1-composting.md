# Standard Operating Procedure: Phase I Yard Composting & Moisture Management (SOP-201)

* **Document ID:** SOP-201
* **Version:** 1.0
* **Target Region:** Global / Commercial Composting Yards
* **Applies To:** Compost Yard Managers, Operations Technicians

---

## 1. Purpose & Scope
This SOP defines the step-by-step management of Phase I outdoor composting, ensuring proper physical breakdown, moisture saturation, and aerobic conditioning of raw agricultural ingredients before tunnel pasteurization.

---

## 2. Raw Material Stacking (Day 0)
1. **Pre-Wet Stack:** Thoroughly pre-wet straw layers using high-volume sprinklers for 24–48 hours until straw reaches saturation.
2. **Layering Formula:** Build alternating layers of wetted straw, animal manure (horse or poultry), agricultural gypsum ($\text{CaSO}_4$), and nitrogen supplements.
3. **Dimensions:** Construct stacks with a rectangular profile (approx. 1.5m to 2m high and wide) to ensure uniform internal heating.

---

## 3. Turning & Moisture Schedule
Execute mechanical or manual turns on the following schedule to maintain aerobic conditions:
* **Day 4 (First Turn):** Break down the outer dry edges, pull material inward, and readjust moisture levels.
* **Day 7 (Second Turn):** Incorporate gypsum evenly throughout the pile; monitor core temperature spikes ($65^\circ\text{C} - 80^\circ\text{C}$).
* **Day 10 (Third Turn):** Assess physical softening and structural dark-brown coloration.
* **Day 13 (Fourth Turn):** Final mixing and moisture check, preparing the substrate for Phase II tunnel transfer.

---

## 4. Quality Control Audit
* [ ] Moisture content verified between $70\% - 75\%$ (squeeze test: few drops released with firm pressure).
* [ ] Core pile temperature verified via probe thermometer ($>65^\circ\text{C}$).
* [ ] Absence of heavy foul odors (indicative of anaerobic pockets; requires immediate turning).
