# Standard Operating Procedure: Integrated Pest Management (IPM) & Hygiene Sanitization (SOP-402)

* **Document ID:** SOP-402
* **Version:** 1.0
* **Target Region:** Global / All Farm Facilities
* **Applies To:** Biosecurity Officers, Farm Workers

---

## 1. Purpose & Scope
This SOP outlines the hygiene protocols, physical barriers, and biological controls required to prevent and remediate pest infestations and pathogen outbreaks.

---

## 2. Biosecurity & Hygiene Protocols
1. **Access Control:** Enforce mandatory footbath disinfection and hand sanitization at all growing room entry points.
2. **Screening & Sealing:** Ensure all air intake vents are fitted with fine mesh screens to block entry by sciarid (*Lycoriella*) and phorid flies.
3. **Post-Crop Cook-Out:** Execute a complete thermal sanitization (cook-out) of growing rooms at the end of a crop cycle before cleaning out spent substrate.

---

## 3. Pathogen & Pest Response
1. **Green Mold & Blotch Management:** Immediately isolate and remove contaminated blocks. Apply targeted sanitation to stop the spread of *Trichoderma* or bacterial blotch (*Pseudomonas tolaasii*).
2. **Fly Monitoring:** Utilize sticky yellow cards throughout growing rooms to monitor insect populations and detect early fly activity.

---

## 4. Quality Control Audit
* [ ] Footbaths checked, cleaned, and refilled with fresh disinfectant daily.
* [ ] Yellow sticky cards inspected and counted weekly for insect trends.
* [ ] Post-crop room heat treatment verified by temperature logs.
