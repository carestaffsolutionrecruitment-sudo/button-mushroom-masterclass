# Standard Operating Procedure: Low-Cost Passive Climate Control & Insulation Setup (SOP-101)

* **Document ID:** SOP-101
* **Version:** 1.0
* **Target Region:** Kenya, East Africa, & Tropical Low-Resource Zones
* **Applies To:** Farm Managers, Infrastructure Builders, Cultivators

---

## 1. Purpose & Scope
This Standard Operating Procedure defines the structural and material requirements for building and managing low-cost, high-performance passive climate-controlled mushroom growing houses. The objective is to maintain optimal thermal mass and relative humidity without relying on expensive, industrial HVAC systems.

---

## 2. Structural Specifications & Materials
* **Wall Construction:** 
  * Use thick mud-daubed walls or **Compressed Stabilized Earth Blocks (CSEB)**. 
  * Wall thickness must be maintained at a minimum of 200mm to provide the thermal mass necessary to buffer daily external temperature swings.
* **Roofing Insulation:** 
  * Install a double-layered grass-thatched roof or an insulated sandwich panel system with an integrated air gap. This acts as a thermal barrier against direct equatorial solar radiation.
* **Flooring:** 
  * Retain bare earthen or compacted screed floors. Wetting the floor during dry, hot cycles provides natural evaporative cooling and helps baseline humidity retention.

---

## 3. Passive Climate Management Protocol
1. **Diurnal Vent Management:** 
   * Open high-level gable vents and low-level intake vents during the coolest hours of the morning (04:00 – 07:00) to flush out accumulated $\text{CO}_2$ and draw in cool air.
   * Seal vents tightly during peak solar radiation hours (11:00 – 15:00) to lock in internal thermal stability.
2. **Evaporative Cooling Curtains:** 
   * Hang damp hessian (burlap) sacks or coconut-fiber mats over intake windows. Use a simple gravity drip-kit pipe to keep them moist, allowing incoming air to pass through and cool down evaporatively.
3. **Humidity Control:** 
   * Perform manual or low-pressure knapsack misting of the walls and floors (avoiding direct contact with developing pins) to maintain baseline Relative Humidity targets of $85\% - 95\%$.

---

## 4. Quality Control & Daily Audit Checklist
* [ ] Internal ambient temperature verified via min/max thermometer ($16^\circ\text{C} - 18^\circ\text{C}$ for fruiting).
* [ ] Earthen floors inspected and watered down if surface drying is detected.
* [ ] Gable and intake vents adjusted according to time-of-day thermal schedules.
* [ ] Burlap cooling curtains checked for continuous moisture flow and absence of mold.
