# Module 5: Agribusiness Economics, Value Addition, & Go-To-Market

## 1. Financial Modeling & Unit Economics
Operating a commercial button mushroom farm requires strict tracking of capital expenditures (CapEx) against recurring operating expenses (OpEx).

* **Infrastructure Investment (CapEx vs. OpEx):**
  * Compare low-budget passive structures (low initial CapEx, higher manual labor OpEx) against automated Dutch-shelf and tunnel systems (high CapEx, optimized labor efficiency, and consistent automated OpEx).
* **Yield Benchmarking:**
  * Calculate and track production efficiency based on yield per square meter ($15 - 25\text{ kg/m}^2$ baseline targets across complete cropping cycles).

---

## 2. Value Addition & Processing Lines
To hedge against fresh market price volatility and reduce post-harvest waste, establish secondary processing workflows:

* **Preservation & Packaging:** Blanching, vacuum-sealing, commercial canning, and low-temperature dehydration lines.
* **Upcycling Off-Spec Produce:** Transform bruised or oversized mushrooms into gourmet mushroom powders, dried slices, or concentrated mushroom-infused broth bases.

---

## 3. Digital Marketing & B2B Sales Funnels
* **B2B Contracts:** Secure recurring supply agreements with local restaurants, hospitality groups, and regional wholesale aggregators.
* **Direct-to-Consumer (D2C):** Implement digital subscription models, online ordering portals, and direct Farmer-to-Market delivery logistics to maximize profit margins.
