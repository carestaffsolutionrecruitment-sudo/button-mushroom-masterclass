## Part 2: Contamination Diagnostic Flowchart

* **Step 1: Symptom Identification**
  * *Powder/Patches Observed:* Is the color Green, Pink, Olive-Green, or Brown?
  * *Malformations Observed:* Are stipes crooked, caps distorted, or gills exposed prematurely?

* **Step 2: Causal Link & Action Plan**
  * **Green Patches (*Trichoderma*):** Caused by unpasteurized pockets or high humidity with stagnant air. *Action:* Isolate block, reduce local humidity, apply selective salt/sanitation treatment.
  * **Bacterial Blotch (Brown sunken spots):** Caused by water droplets remaining on caps $>4$ hours. *Action:* Adjust FAE drying speed, reduce misting duration.
  * **Pest Damage (Sciarid/Phorid Larva):** Holes in stems or mycelium eaten away. *Action:* Check intake filters, deploy yellow sticky cards, verify post-crop cook-out protocols.
