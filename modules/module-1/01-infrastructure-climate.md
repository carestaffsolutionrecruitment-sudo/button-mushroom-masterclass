# Module 1: Global Infrastructure & Climate Control Engineering

## 1. Regional Adaptation Frameworks
Designing a commercial button mushroom (*Agaricus bisporus*) production facility requires balancing strict environmental parameters with local climatic realities and economic constraints.

### Kenya & East Africa
* **Structure Type:** Passive low-budget and semi-automated growing structures. 
* **Materials:** Utilizing thick mud-daubed or compressed stabilized earth block (CSEB) walls to provide massive thermal mass, buffering against diurnal temperature swings. 
* **Roofing & Flooring:** Double-layered grass-thatched roofs (or insulated sandwich panels) to deflect intense equatorial solar heat. Earthen or screed floors are maintained to assist with natural evaporative cooling and baseline humidity retention.
* **Geographic Optimization:** Exploiting natural high-altitude geographic advantages (such as Kiambu, Nyeri, and Limuru) where ambient baseline temperatures naturally approximate optimal fruiting zones.

### West Africa
* **Thermal Management:** Coping with high tropical ambient temperatures year-round.
* **Strain Selection:** Integrating warm-weather button strains such as *Agaricus bitorquis*, which tolerate higher temperatures up to $25^\circ\text{C} - 26^\circ\text{C}$.
* **Infrastructure Design:** Utilizing low-cost root-cellar concepts, double-walled shade houses, and roof-thatch drip-kit systems for continuous evaporative cooling over the building envelope.

### Europe & USA
* **Industrial Facilities:** High-tech insulated Dutch-style growing rooms constructed from aluminum-insulated sandwich panels.
* **Automation:** Fully integrated Air Handling Units (AHUs), closed-loop chillers, automated negative/positive pressure plenums, and computerized SCADA-based climate controllers governing every stage of growth.

## 2. Environmental Physics & HVAC Configuration

### Target Operational Parameters Across Cycles
* **Spawn Run Stage:** 
  * Compost Temperature: $24^\circ\text{C} - 25^\circ\text{C}$
  * Relative Humidity (RH): $90\% - 95\%$
  * $\text{CO}_2$ Concentration: $5,000 - 10,000\text{ ppm}$
* **Pinning & Fruiting Stage:** 
  * Air Temperature: $16^\circ\text{C} - 18^\circ\text{C}$
  * Relative Humidity (RH): $85\% - 90\%$
  * Fresh Air Exchanges (FAEs): $4 - 6$ complete air changes per hour
  * $\text{CO}_2$ Concentration: Reduced below $1,000\text{ ppm}$

### Air Distribution & Filtration Mechanics
* **Plenums & Ducting:** Perforated overhead or floor-level ducts designed to ensure uniform laminar airflow across all tiers of growing shelves or bags.
* **Filtration:** Positive pressure air filtration systems utilizing HEPA and pre-filters to prevent the ingress of competitor mold spores, dust, and sciarid flies.
* **Thermal Baffling:** Anti-condensation thermal baffling to prevent moisture from dripping directly onto developing mushroom pins, which causes bacterial blotch.
