# Global Commercial Button Mushroom (*Agaricus bisporus*) Production Masterclass

A comprehensive, industrial-grade engineering and agribusiness masterclass for commercial cultivators, agronomists, and enterprise founders. It connects automated Dutch-shelf technology (Europe/USA) and hot-tropical adaptations (West Africa) with robust, low-cost passive climate systems, and it gives a **complete, locally optimised operating blueprint for commercial production in Kenya and East Africa**.

---

## 🇰🇪 Regional Focus: Commercial Production in Kenya & East Africa

While maintaining global best practices, this masterclass provides specialised engineering and supply-chain protocols for the East African agricultural landscape:

* **Agro-climatic optimisation:** Kenya's high-altitude zones (Limuru, Kiambu, Nyeri, Eldoret, Timau) have mean temperatures that already match the 16–18 °C fruiting target. Thermal-mass buildings damp the diurnal swing so *Agaricus bisporus* can fruit without chillers.
* **Local substrate sourcing:** Rift Valley wheat and barley straw, poultry manure from Kienyeji and commercial operations, gypsum from Kajiado, and sustainable bulking agents such as sugarcane bagasse and coffee husks, with lab-verifiable recipes.
* **Water security and off-grid engineering:** borehole development (WRA permitting), solar-powered pumping sized from first principles, rainwater harvesting, and groundwater pH/alkalinity buffering.
* **Regulatory and agribusiness ecosystem:** NEMA environmental licensing, KEBS standardisation, KRA tax obligations (eTIMS, TOT/CIT, VAT), county and DOSHS requirements, and financing through SACCOs, AFC, banks, and development programmes.

## 📖 Curriculum Breakdown

### Start Here — Kenya Regional Reference Annex (KE-00)
The single source of truth for Kenyan climate data by zone, the raw-material sourcing directory (with indicative KES prices), water and energy engineering, the regulatory compliance map, the tax quick-reference, and the finance ecosystem.

### Module 1: Facility Infrastructure & Climate Engineering
* Site selection scorecard, dirty-to-clean biosecure layout, and east–west orientation for equatorial sun.
* Low-cost passive structures: CSEB, machine-cut stone, and treated-timber walls; insulated iron-sheet roofs with radiant barriers or double-layer grass thatch; earth-air tubes; night-flush ventilation; evaporative curtains.
* Industrial and semi-industrial HVAC: fan sizing with altitude correction, cooling-load estimates, filtration, and an instrument kit.

### Module 2: Substrate Engineering & Phase I/II Composting
* Recipe formulation by C:N (≈ 30:1 falling to ≈ 20:1) **and** nitrogen % of dry matter, with a Kenyan recipe library (Rift Standard, Broiler Blend, Kienyeji Cooperative, Coffee Belt).
* Moisture control (70–75%), roofed Phase I yards for two rainy seasons, and leachate management (NEMA).
* Phase II in industrial tunnels or low-cost Kenyan chambers: pasteurisation, conditioning (NH₃ < 150 ppm milestone, ≤ 5–10 ppm release), and the cooperative hub-and-spoke compost model.

### Module 3: Aseptic Spawning, Incubation, & Casing Science
* Spawn sourcing (local labs vs KEPHIS-permitted imports) and the road cold-chain protocol for Kenyan routes.
* Spawning at 0.5–1.0%, and spawn-run control that keeps compost below the lethal 27 °C through highland heat and cold extremes.
* Casing from peat alternatives (coir, red volcanic subsoil, sandy loam), buffered to pH 7.2–7.5 with agricultural limestone using a lime jar test.

### Module 4: Fruiting, Pests, Diseases, Harvesting, & Post-Harvest
* Pinning induction (16–18 °C, CO₂ < 1,000 ppm, high fresh air), including a passive night-flush shock for highland rooms.
* IPM for sciarid/phorid flies and disease control (green mould, blotch, dry/wet bubble, cobweb), with a Kenyan seasonal risk calendar.
* Harvesting and grading (Button, Cup, Flats; UNECE FFV-24), and cooling to 2–4 °C at 90% RH with workarounds for power and transport gaps.

### Module 5: Agribusiness Economics, Value Addition, & Go-To-Market
* KES/USD financial models: a highland passive farm (CapEx ≈ KES 14.5M; break-even ≈ 11 kg/m²; payback ≈ 3.5 years simple, ≈ 4.75–5.3 years with ramp-up and tax) and a semi-industrial reference.
* Yield benchmarking (15–25 kg/m²) and channels: supermarkets (Naivas, Quickmart, and others), open-air wholesale markets, hospitality, D2C, regional and export routes.
* Value addition (dried, powder, SMC), cooperative economics, financing, the compliance calendar, and a risk register.

## 🖥️ Publishing on ProductDyno Pro

The markdown files are the source of truth. ProductDyno's lesson editor does not render Markdown, LaTeX math, or diagram code, so:

* All content uses **plain Unicode** for units and formulas (°C, CO₂, NH₃, m³, ≤, ×). Nothing needs a math renderer.
* `productdyno/` holds **paste-ready HTML** for every lesson, with styles written inline so they survive the editor.

**Workflow**
1. Edit the markdown in `modules/` or `assets/`.
2. Regenerate the HTML: `pip install markdown && python3 tools/build_productdyno.py`
3. In ProductDyno, open the lesson → switch the content editor to its **source/HTML view** → paste the matching file from `productdyno/` → save. Open `productdyno/index.html` in a browser to preview the whole course locally.

**Suggested ProductDyno course structure** (the order used by the build script):

| Section | Lessons |
| :--- | :--- |
| Start Here | KE-00 Kenya Regional Reference |
| Module 1 | Infrastructure & Climate · SOP-101 · Deliverable-101 |
| Module 2 | Substrate & Composting · SOP-201 · SOP-202 · Deliverable-202 |
| Module 3 | Spawning & Casing · SOP-301 · SOP-302 · Deliverable-303 |
| Module 4 | Fruiting & Harvest · SOP-401 · SOP-402 · SOP-403 · Deliverable-404 |
| Module 5 | Economics & Markets · Deliverable-505 |
| Bonus | Kenya Institutional Funding Frameworks (YEDF, WEF, Uwezo, Hustler Fund, AFC, KCIC, bank agribusiness divisions) |

## 🔎 Version 2.0 Audit — Technical Corrections

| Area | Correction |
| :--- | :--- |
| Repository | Files had been created in a recursively nested path (`modules/module-1/modules/module-2/…/assets/sops/assets/sops/…`). The layout is flattened to the structure above and module files are numbered consistently (01–05). |
| Phase II (Module 2, SOP-202) | Stage order corrected to fill → level → **pasteurise → condition** → cool. NH₃ < 150 ppm is now the conditioning milestone; spawning requires ≤ 5–10 ppm. |
| Grow-room floors (Module 1, SOP-101) | Bare earthen floors are removed from grow rooms (they harbour nematodes, flies, and moulds and cannot be disinfected). Specified sealed screed with drains instead. |
| Biological efficiency (Deliverable-404) | BE is now calculated on compost **dry** weight. The old wet-weight formula is relabelled as yield per tonne. |
| C:N formulation (Module 2, Deliverable-202) | Added the N%-of-dry-matter check alongside C:N, because ash-heavy manures can meet C:N and still be nitrogen-starved. |
| Altitude (all climate documents) | Added air-density fan correction and NDIR CO₂ pressure correction for 1,700–2,500 m sites. |
