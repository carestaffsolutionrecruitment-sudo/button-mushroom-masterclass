Working-capital rule: hold ≥ 3 months of OpEx (reference ≈ KES 1.7M), because supermarket payment terms of 30–60+ days and the 2-month gap from spawning to first harvest consume cash before revenue arrives.

Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) = annual operating profit ÷ annual loan repayments. Lenders typically look for ≥ 1.3–1.5.
