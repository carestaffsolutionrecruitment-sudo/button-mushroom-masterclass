# Deliverable Template: Commercial Farm Business Plan & Financial Feasibility Model (Deliverable-505)

* **Template ID:** Deliverable-505
* **Version:** 2.0
* **Purpose:** A bank- and investor-ready planning framework for a commercial button mushroom enterprise. It models CapEx, OpEx, yields, channel revenue, break-even, payback, sensitivity, and 5-year cash flow in **Kenyan Shillings (KES)** with **USD** equivalents. A completed Kenyan reference case (highland passive farm, Track K1) is included alongside blank input columns.
* **Linked Documents:** Module 5 · Deliverable-202 (raw-material costs) · Deliverable-404 (actual yields) · KE-00 §5–6

> **How to use:** keep the "Reference" column as a sanity check and fill the "Your farm" column with your own quotes. All reference values are **indicative 2025/26 planning figures**. Replace them before submitting to a lender.

## 6. Five-Year Cash-Flow Projection (base case)

Ramp-up: Year 1 = 70%, Year 2 = 90%, Years 3–5 = 100% of base yield. OpEx is held constant (conservative: Year-1 costs are carried in full while yields ramp up).

| Year | Production (kg) | Revenue (KES) | OpEx (KES) | Operating profit (KES) | Tax — TOT 1.5% (KES) | Cumulative cash — TOT (KES) | Tax — 30% CIT (KES) | Cumulative cash — CIT (KES) |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0 | — | — | — | — | — | −14,520,000 | — | −14,520,000 |
| 1 | 15,120 | 7,665,840 | 6,820,080 | 845,760 | 114,988 | −13,789,228 | 0 | −13,674,240 |

| 4 | 21,600 | 10,951,200 | 6,820,080 | 4,131,120 | 164,268 | −2,967,365 | 803,736 | −4,458,672 |
| 5 | 21,600 | 10,951,200 | 6,820,080 | 4,131,120 | 164,268 | **+999,487** | 803,736 | −1,131,288 |

* **TOT column:** Turnover Tax at 1.5% of gross revenue (applies to turnover in the KES 1M–25M band; KE-00 §5.1).
* **CIT column:** 30% × (operating profit − 10-year straight-line depreciation of CapEx); no loss carry-forward modelled.
* **Payback with ramp-up and tax: ≈ 4.75 years (TOT) to ≈ 5.3 years (CIT).**
* Financing costs are not included. Add loan interest and repayments in §7.
* Upside not modelled: SMC sales (≈ 6 t per crop at KES 1,500–4,000/t), value-added dried products, and seasonal December price premiums.

**Working-capital rule:** hold ≥ 3 months of OpEx (reference ≈ KES 1.7M), because supermarket payment terms of 30–60+ days and the 2-month gap from spawning to first harvest consume cash before revenue arrives.

**Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR)** = annual operating profit ÷ annual loan repayments. Lenders typically look for ≥ 1.3–1.5.
