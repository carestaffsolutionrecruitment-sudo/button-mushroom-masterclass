# Standard Operating Procedure: Pinning Induction & Shock Phase Control (SOP-401)

* **Document ID:** SOP-401
* **Version:** 2.0
* **Target Region:** Global growing-room operations, with Kenyan passive-room procedures
* **Applies To:** Climate Control Technicians, Growing Supervisors
* **Linked Documents:** Module 4 §1 · SOP-101 · Deliverable-101 · KE-00 §1.4

---

## 1. Purpose & Scope

This SOP defines the environmental shock that moves a cased room from vegetative growth (case run) to uniform primordia (pinhead) formation, in both controller-managed rooms and passive highland rooms.

---

## 2. Start Criteria

* [ ] Mycelium visible at the casing surface over **≈ 70%** of the area
* [ ] Casing at field capacity (no further heavy watering during the shock)
* [ ] Room, doors, and screens checked; yellow sticky traps fresh
* [ ] Weather forecast checked (passive rooms): start on a cool night

---

## 3. Shock Parameters

| Parameter | Start (case run) | Target (pinning) | Ramp |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Air temperature | 22–24 °C | **16–18 °C** | Over **24–48 h** |
| Compost temperature | 23–25 °C | 17–19 °C | Follows the air |
| CO₂ | 3,000–5,000 ppm | **< 1,000 ppm** (altitude-corrected) | Within 24 h |
| RH | 90–95% | **85–90%** | Within 24 h |
| Fresh air | Low | **4–6 air changes/h** | Step up |

---

## 4. Procedure

### 4.1 Controller-managed rooms (global / Track K2)

1. Step the air setpoint down by **1–2 °C per 12 h** until 16–18 °C.
2. Raise fresh air to bring CO₂ < 1,000 ppm.
3. Set RH to 85–90%; check that casing evaporation is gentle (surface slightly matt, not shiny-wet, not dry/pale).
4. Hold for 5–7 days; pins appear about **7–10 days** after the shock starts.
5. Watering: none or very light misting until pins reach pea size (5–8 mm).

### 4.2 Passive highland rooms (Track K1)

1. **Night 1 (≈ 20:00):** open low intakes, earth tubes, and high outlets fully. The building mass dumps its heat overnight.
2. **Morning:** record temperature, RH, and CO₂ at 07:00. Target ≤ 18 °C air.
3. **Day:** keep earth-tube fans running; throttle main vents during the afternoon peak (11:00–16:00) just enough to hold CO₂ < 1,000 ppm; wet curtains in the dry seasons only.
4. **Nights 2–3:** repeat the night flush.
5. **Cold-site variant** (Timau / upper Molo / Jun–Aug): if night air falls below 10 °C, throttle the night intakes to stop the room going below 14 °C, and do more of the ventilation in late morning.
6. **Humidity:** mist walls/floors (never the casing directly) to hold 85–90%.

### 4.3 Readings

Every **4–6 h for the first 72 h**, then twice daily: air temperature, compost temperature, RH, CO₂ (apply the altitude correction reading × 1,013 / P(site) if the meter is not pressure-compensated; at ≈ 780 hPa multiply by ≈ 1.3).

---

## 5. Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | Corrective action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| No pins after 10–12 days | CO₂ still > 1,500 ppm; temperature never reached ≤ 18 °C; casing too dry | Verify with a corrected CO₂ reading; extend night flushing; light watering if the casing is pale/dry |
| Dense clusters of tiny pins (overpinning) | Shock too abrupt; casing surface too wet; temperature dropped too far | Slower ramp next time; reduce surface wetness; hold 17–18 °C |
| Pins form below the casing surface | Casing too deep; CO₂ too high at the surface | Check depth (3–5 cm); increase surface air movement |
| Pins dry out / turn brown | RH < 82%; strong direct air on beds; dry-season air | Raise RH; redirect airflow off the bed surface; wet curtains |
| Pins rot / bacterial spotting | Water on pins; RH > 95%; condensation drip | Stop watering onto pins; increase air movement; fix ceiling baffles |
| Pins only on part of the room | Uneven airflow; temperature stratification | Rearrange or add recirculation (poly-tube duct); check that the top tiers are not warmer |
| Room won't fall below 19 °C (hot season, passive) | Hot nights; weak night flush | Start the shock during a cool spell; add an inline fan to earth tubes; consider seasonal crop scheduling (avoid pinning in the hottest weeks) |

---

## 6. Quality Control Audit

* [ ] Air temperature stable at **16–18 °C**
* [ ] CO₂ **< 1,000 ppm** (altitude-corrected value recorded)
* [ ] RH **85–90%** without water-logging the casing surface
* [ ] Readings logged every 4–6 h for 72 h
* [ ] Pin date and pin uniformity score (1–5) recorded on Deliverable-404
